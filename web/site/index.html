Independent review

01•Model jury

The default jury contains two large generalists from different lineages, a document specialist, a small fast model and a dissenter. Each receives the same source and versioned task schema. The protocol records structured fields, confidence and evidence hashes before comparing results.

Explore the protocol

MOCHI is in development. The dashboard demonstrates the intended review flow with clearly labelled sample data.

Evidence first

02•Cited fields

A field-level verdict contains the modal answer, the agreement share, evidence references and minority answers. If a required field remains below its configured threshold, the overall status is HUNG. Model consensus is not proof that the source or answer is correct.

View a sample verdict

Inspect a sample Decision Packet in the dashboard. No demonstration result is a live model run or an on-chain attestation.

Private by design

03•Controlled access

The proposed live protocol encrypts documents to enclave keys and provides payer-controlled evidence access. This frontend does not upload or encrypt documents to live enclaves. Its local previews help developers implement and test those states before production integration.

Read the integration notes

Production launch requires enclave attestation validation, authentication, secure key management and audited access controls.

Fail closed

04•Honest outcomes

A HUNG result remains unresolved until the requester chooses a larger jury, a human panel or acceptance of the split. Feeds must reject unresolved or cross-check-failed updates and expose the timestamp of the last accepted verdict.

How It Works

●

Section

The Process:

Description

01

Choose a claim

Paste one factual claim or provide supporting source URLs. Keep the date and scope clear, and consent to external research before submission.

02

Review independently

Inspect the evidence bundle first. Three different configured models then assess the claim independently against retrieved sources.

03

Assess the findings

See whether evidence supports, contradicts or adds context to the claim. Missing evidence and jury disagreement stay visible; agreement does not guarantee correctness.

04

Retain the evidence

Download the research result or explicitly publish a share link. The pilot record includes sources and model findings; it is not an enclave attestation or chain receipt.
