BEFORE YOU SHARE IT

Read the claim.
Check the evidence.

Three model jurors review a shared evidence bundle. You see their findings, citations and disagreements—and decide what the evidence supports.

Available now: invitation research. No payment is collected. Paid confidential reviews are not open.

01 / GET STARTED

One claim at a time.

  1. Make the claim specific.

    Paste one factual statement from a public post or article. Include the date, version or qualifier that makes it testable. Keep it under 4,000 characters.

  2. Bring the sources.

    Add up to five public HTTPS links to readable HTML or plain text. Source discovery is not currently configured, so include links. Private posts, PDFs, sign-in pages and paywalled content are not supported.

  3. Inspect before reviewing.

    Enter your invitation token and consent to external processing. Check the retrieved passages and warnings before starting the three jurors. Never submit confidential or personal material.

  4. Read beyond the label.

    Follow each citation to its source. All three valid findings must agree for a resolved assessment; two votes are not enough. A matching quote does not prove the conclusion is correct.

  5. Save or share deliberately.

    Download the result. Publishing separately exposes the exact claim, findings, cited passages and source metadata to anyone with the link. Save your private owner recovery details to manage the publication later.

02 / FRAME THE QUESTION

Give the jury something to verify.

A product announcement

“Version 2.1 supports feature X on the free plan.”

Supply the dated release note and plan documentation. An announcement of a future feature does not establish that it is available now.

A crypto claim

“This contract lets its administrator mint additional tokens.”

Supply verified source documentation and identify the chain and contract. This research tool does not replace a contract audit or investment analysis.

An AI comparison

“Model A beat model B on benchmark C under these settings.”

Include the evaluation table, metric, model versions and methodology. A narrow benchmark does not establish that a model is best at everything.

These are example question formats, not completed reviews or claims about particular products.

03 / UNDERSTAND THE RESULT

Agreement is evidence to inspect.

Supported / contradicted
The jurors agree that the supplied evidence supports or conflicts with the exact claim.
Missing context
Qualifications or omitted context materially change the interpretation.
Insufficient evidence
The supplied material does not establish an answer. With no usable source text, the service returns this assessment without calling the models.
Unresolved
The three valid findings did not agree, or one or more jurors failed. Missing responses are not votes.

Research results are unattested research. Their integrity hash binds the record; it is not proof of truth, an on-chain receipt or an end-to-end confidentiality guarantee.

04 / QUESTIONS BEFORE LAUNCH

Know the limits.

Does it cost ten cents today?

No. The invitation pilot is unpaid. The planned short three-juror tariff starts at $0.10 in USDG for the minimum quote tier, before network gas. Larger inputs can cost more; complete cost validation and production activation remain required. An on-chain quote will govern a paid request.

What will happen if a paid review fails?

Funds are escrowed when a paid query opens. Under the current contracts, HUNG refunds the protocol fee and timed-out juror fees; responding jurors still earn their fees. Expiry refunds remaining escrow. A completed verdict, including an insufficient-evidence verdict, is chargeable. There is no cancellation refund after opening, and network gas is not refunded by MOCHI. These describe the future paid path, not the free pilot.

Can I correct or withdraw a shared result?

For publications created with owner controls, save the owner token privately. Use Manage publication with your invitation token and owner token to add a dated owner correction or unpublish. Corrections do not rewrite the original jury findings. Unpublishing removes access through MOCHI; it cannot erase copies others already saved. Older publications may lack recoverable ownership.

What is retained?

Private evidence sessions expire after 30 minutes and private results after 60 minutes; a service restart ends both. Public shares and aggregate usage persist. Owner controls are separate from the private review session. Source text is omitted from public shares except for cited passages.

Is this confidential document review?

No. The pilot processes public claims and sources through external providers, and the website proxy can see requests. The separate encrypted protocol has been rehearsed, but paid production access is not active. Do not put private documents into this research form.

Do reviews automatically burn MOCHI?

Not during the pilot. The implemented purchase path remains disabled until launch configuration is approved. The proposed policy uses eligible review-fee surplus after obligations and reserves, with a $25 purchase minimum. Burns are manual. Developer fees stay separate. Read the tokenomics proposal ↗
