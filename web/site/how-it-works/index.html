How It Works

Section

The Process:

Description

01

Write the claim, add sources

Paste one exact claim and one to five source excerpts, each with a title and HTTPS link. The jurors see only the text you paste; links are labels and are never fetched.

02

Encrypt and pay

Your browser checks the intake enclave’s attestation, then encrypts the claim and excerpts to it before they leave the page. You pay the on-chain quote, from $0.10 USDG; network gas is separate.

03

The attested jury reviews

Qwen 3.6, Gemma 4 and GPT-OSS 120B review your excerpts through attested confidential inference, each quoting its evidence verbatim. All three must agree; otherwise the result is HUNG.

04

Decrypt and verify

The result is encrypted to your key. Your browser decrypts it and checks it against the answer hash recorded on chain. Your payment, the query record and the result hashes are public.

2:

When Models Disagree

●

Section

Outcomes:

Description

VERDICT or HUNG.

Each juror assesses the exact claim against your excerpts: supported, contradicted, missing context or insufficient evidence. At launch the jury has three AI jurors, and all three must agree on one outcome. Larger juries, up to 9, are configurable later.

VERDICT

All three jurors agree on one outcome, each quoting its evidence verbatim. Your browser shows it after checking the on-chain answer hash.

HUNG

HUNG means the jurors did not agree; no outcome is presented. Human panels are not available at launch.

The jurors judge only the excerpts you paste. Agreement shows that three models read the same evidence the same way; it does not prove that the claim or the source is correct.
