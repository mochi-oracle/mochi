# What is mocked (and what replaces it before mainnet)

Switch with `TEE_MODE=tdx` (configfs-tsm) or `TEE_MODE=dstack` (Phala Cloud / dstack CVMs) + `QUOTE_VERIFIER=dcap`. **Verified on real hardware 2026-09-27**: a Phala Cloud TDX CVM's fresh quote passed against Intel's live collateral (UpToDate) and its measurement is registered on RHC testnet. Still missing: packaging the Mochi services themselves as CVM images, NVIDIA GPU attestation (`NrasQuoteVerifier` stub), AMD SEV-SNP |
